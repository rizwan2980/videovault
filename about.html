About VideoVault

Your personal video library — every great video, one tap away.

VideoVault is a hand-picked library of video links from across the internet — YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Dailymotion, Bilibili and more — organised in one clean, premium place.

How it works

  • Plays inside first: where a platform offers an official embedded player, the video plays right here on VideoVault — no tab-hopping.
  • One tap to the source: where in-site play is not offered, the card opens the video on its original website.
  • Your own shelf: add any link yourself, mark favourites, set your own banner pictures, and back them up — stored privately in your browser.
  • Shared Library: a common catalogue, curated by the owner, that every visitor sees.

Who runs VideoVault

VideoVault is created and curated by Rizwan Saeed, a creator from Pakistan behind the "Learn & Earn" brand — teaching people how to learn digital tools and earn online through Facebook, YouTube and TikTok. VideoVault grew out of a simple problem: good videos get lost in feeds. This library keeps them found.

Respect for creators

Every video here belongs to its original creator and platform, and plays through the platform's own player — views count where they should. Rights holders can reach us any time via the Contact page; our takedown process is described in the Terms of Use.

Get in touch

Suggest a video for the Shared Library, report a broken link, or ask anything: use the Contact page.
