Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026 · Last updated: 26 October 2026

VideoVault (https://rizwan2980.github.io/videovault/) is a video link library operated by Rizwan Saeed ("we", "us"). This policy explains, in plain language, what information is involved when you use this website, and the choices you have. If anything is unclear, contact us at muhammadrizwansaeed85@gmail.com.

1. What VideoVault is

VideoVault is a catalogue of links to videos that live on other platforms (such as YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Dailymotion and Bilibili). Videos are played through those platforms' own official embedded players, or opened on the platform's own website. We do not host, copy or re-upload the videos themselves.

2. Information stored only in your own browser

Some features save information in your own browser's local storage, on your own device:

  • videos you add yourself ("My Additions"),
  • your favourites, and any banner pictures you set,
  • your cookie notice choice.

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us, and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time by clearing your browser's site data, or by removing the items inside the site. A video file you choose with the upload option is played inside your browser only — it is never uploaded to any server.

3. Information collected automatically by hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like almost every web host, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to operate and secure the service. Those logs are governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not operate user accounts on this website and we do not ask for names, passwords or payment details.

4. Third-party embedded videos and their cookies

When you press play, the video is delivered by the platform that hosts it, through its official player. That platform may set cookies and collect usage data under its own privacy policy (for example Google/YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, Meta for Instagram and Facebook videos, Dailymotion, or Bilibili). We do not control those cookies. When you follow a link to the original website, that website's own privacy policy applies from that point.

5. Advertising and advertising cookies

We may display advertising on this website, including ads served by Google AdSense and other advertising partners. Advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads, including ads personalised to your interests based on your visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or via aboutads.info/choices. Visitors in Europe can also use youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Where the law requires consent for advertising cookies (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), ads that require consent are shown only in line with the consent choices presented on this site.
  • Opting out of personalised ads does not remove ads; it means the ads you see may be less relevant to you.

6. Analytics

If we use visit statistics in future, they will be used only in aggregate form (for example, how many people visited and which categories are popular) so we can improve the library. We do not build profiles of individual visitors and we never sell personal information.

7. Children

This website is a general-audience library and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Your rights and choices

Because your additions and preferences live only in your browser, you control them fully and can delete them at any time as described in section 2. For any other privacy question, or to exercise rights you may have under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, write to us at muhammadrizwansaeed85@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.
