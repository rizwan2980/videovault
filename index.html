How it works: the link becomes a card with a picture, title and site badge. THE ORDER (owner's choice): if a video CAN play inside this website, clicking its card plays it HERE first. Only when a site offers no in-site player does clicking open the video on its own website instead — and the card says so before you click. Pictures + the real title now come automatically for YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok and Dailymotion — fetched from the site itself a second after you add the link. Instagram, Facebook and Bilibili keep their pictures locked away from other websites, so those cards show the title as their poster unless you paste a poster image link yourself. Sites with an in-site player (YouTube, Vimeo, Drive, bilibili.com, direct .mp4...) carry the big gold ▶ Play here button. Watch opens the original site WITHOUT telling it where the click came from (no-referrer), because a few strict sites (e.g. Bilibili, guarded by Akamai) sometimes block visits that arrive from inside other apps — if one still shows an error page, copy its link (⧉ button) and paste it directly into Chrome.
